# Guide — features

> **TL;DR** — a spec names **capabilities** (`features`), a spec chooses **field types**, and an
> **archetype** (`pattern`) decides the shape. Nothing is emitted unless it is in the registry
> with an emitter and a check.

Generated by `kiln guide` from `catalogue/` at 1.0.2. Do not edit this file.

---

## The three layers

| layer | lives in | a spec names it as |
|---|---|---|
| capability | `catalogue/capabilities.json` | `features: ["crud", …]` |
| field type | `catalogue/features/field-*.json` | `entities[].fields[].type` |
| archetype | `catalogue/patterns/*.json` | `pattern: "crud-list"` |

| `search` | Search across declared fields | crud | typing a query that no record contains hides every record |
| `sequence` | An explicit order, with the position and the running time | crud | three tracks of 3, 4 and 5 minutes show 3 of 3 and 12 minutes in total |
| `sort` | Sort by any column | crud | sorting by a field reorders the rendered list |
| `split` | One amount divided across rows by weight, with nothing lost to rounding | crud | an amount of 100 split by 1, 1 and 1 shows 34, 33 and 33 |
| `stats` | Count and summary strip | crud | the summary count equals the number of records rendered |
| `steps` | A form in steps, validated one step at a time | crud | a three step wizard shows Back only from the second step and Save only on the last |
| `tags` | Tag chips on each record | crud | a record saved with two tags shows two chips |
| `tally` | A per-row count, with the leader marked | crud | options with 5, 3 and 2 votes show 10 total and the 5 marked as leading |
| `theme` | Dark and light theme, remembered | — | toggling the theme and reloading keeps the chosen theme |
| `timeline` | The list grouped by month, newest first | crud | two records in November and one in October show two month headings |
| `toggle` | Toggle a boolean from the row, without opening the form | crud | ticking the box on a row saves immediately and moves the progress bar |
| `totals` | A running total of one numeric field | crud | two entries of 30 and 90 minutes show a total of 2h |
| `variance` | Planned against actual, with what is left | crud | planned 5000 and actual 3500 show 1500 remaining and a bar two thirds full |
| `versus` | Two records side by side, field by field | crud | two records differing on a price show which is cheaper and which fields match |

## The rule that keeps this honest

| If you add | You must also add | Enforced by |
|---|---|---|
| a field type to the runtime | a `catalogue/features/field-*.json` with `status: emitted` | guard `C14` |
| a capability | an assertion, a runtime gate, and a row in `PROMPT.md` | `catalog.load()`, guard `B6` |
| an archetype | a descriptor, a sample spec, a screenshot, a pros/cons block | guard `C15` |
